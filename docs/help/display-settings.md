# Display settings

The **Display** tab has the settings of *Settings > System > Display* per monitor: resolution, refresh rate,
orientation, scale, HDR, brightness (external monitors through DDC/CI), colour profile and Win+P. A new
resolution or arrangement is undone after **15 seconds** unless you keep it. Windows only.

**While the game runs:** tick *Use these settings while <game> runs* and choose what to change (*Keep* leaves
a setting alone). They are applied on **Start** / **Restart**, before the game starts, and undone when it
closes, on **Restore now**, or when WindowManager closes (after a crash: at the next start).
Games started outside the app are not affected.
