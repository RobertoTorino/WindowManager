# Fit to monitor

Makes the game's *picture* fill a monitor exactly. After **Start** the app waits until the game's window has
settled (about two seconds unchanged), then opens the popup. Open it any time with **Ctrl+F**.

- Choose the **monitor** and **full monitor** (true borderless) or **work area** (taskbar visible).
- Keep **Remove title bar and borders first** ticked: it fits exactly, also on a second monitor.
- **Apply correction** moves and resizes the window, checks up to three times, and says whether it fits.
  A borderless result is saved, so **Force saved settings** restores it.
- The popup blocks clicks on the main window: close it first.
- Turn the automatic popup off under *View > Check window fit after launching a game*.

Fitting changes only the window, not the game's render resolution.
